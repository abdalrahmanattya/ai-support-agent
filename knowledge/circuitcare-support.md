## Returns and refunds

- Standard unopened products can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- Opened headphones and other personal electronics can be returned within 15 days.
- Items must include their accessories and serial-number labels.
- Card refunds normally appear in 3–5 business days; bank refunds can take 10 business days.
- Damaged lithium batteries must not be shipped. Contact a human support specialist for safe handling.

## Loyalty program

- Silver: 0–999 points and no tier discount.
- Gold: 1,000–4,999 points and a 10% discount after points redemption.
- Platinum: 5,000 or more points and a 15% discount after points redemption.
- 100 points equal $1. The minimum redemption is 500 points.
- Points are redeemed in blocks of 500 and may cover at most half of an order.
- Standard products earn one point per dollar; CircuitCare devices earn two points per dollar.

## Order statuses

- PROCESSING: confirmed and being prepared.
- SHIPPED: handed to the carrier.
- OUT_FOR_DELIVERY: with the local delivery driver.
- DELIVERED: carrier confirms delivery.
- CANCELLED: cancelled and any eligible refund has started.

## Escalation

Escalate battery damage, safety hazards, account compromise, charge disputes, legal requests, and any
case where the order tools disagree with the customer. The agent must not promise compensation or
override policy.
